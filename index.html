About Us

Who we are

We are a group of passionate Nomash students, united by the love we share for solving problems and bringing solutions. Our club brings together students from all faculties, who share a love for engineering.

Our goal

We at the Nomash Engineers provide fun, useful and collaborative ways for students to solve a vast array of issues.

What we do

Heres a dive into what we get up to at the Nomash Engineers.

Learning

Joining us gives you millions of learning opportunities, from seminars and workshops to industry experiences, you are bound to gain new knowledge.

Collaboration

We host collaborations with other clubs, other unis and other companies all the time.
