About Us

Who we are

We are a group of passionate Nomash students, united by the love we share for solving problems and bringing solutions. Our club brings together students from all faculties, who share a love for engineering.

\

Our goal

We at the Nomash Engineers provide fun, useful and collabarative ways for students to solve a vast array of issues.

How we started

The Nomash Engineers were started in 2016, where a group of get going Nomash students together formed a team, that would eventually grow into so much more.

Learning
